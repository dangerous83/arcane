THE COMPANY / ESTABLISHED 2004

Craftsmanship built
on experience.

Arcane Interior Decorations brings specialist skill and engineering discipline to interiors across the UAE.

From sculptural gypsum forms to coordinated finishing packages, we work with architects, developers and project teams to translate design intent into a carefully executed interior.

Our experience spans hospitality, residential, commercial and public-sector environments, with skilled people and detailed quality inspection at the heart of the work.
