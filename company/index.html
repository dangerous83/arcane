INTERIOR DECORATION IN DUBAI & THE UAE

Established in 2004, Arcane Interior Decorations combines specialist craftsmanship with engineering discipline for interior projects across the United Arab Emirates.

A specialist partner for the complete interior

The shape of a ceiling, the line of a partition and the meeting point between architectural surfaces all contribute to how a space feels. Arcane brings these specialist elements together through Sculptural GRG, coordinated ceiling systems and precisely resolved partitions.

We work with architects, developers, contractors and project teams to translate design intent into carefully executed interior packages. Our focus is the specialist work within a project: understanding the required scope, coordinating the interfaces and applying the right craft to each finishing layer.

Craftsmanship supported by technical coordination

Traditional plaster-casting skills and modern mould development form the foundation of our Sculptural GRG capability. Curved surfaces, decorative columns, mouldings, sculptural ceilings and wall panels call for close attention to form, assembly and the final surface.

Our ceiling capability covers gypsum, metal, acoustic, cement board and HPL applications. Our partition expertise includes gypsum partitions, wall cladding, cement board, glass and composite systems. Each capability addresses a different layer of the interior while sharing a considered approach to coordination and inspection.

Experience across the UAE project landscape

The company portfolio spans hospitality, residential, commercial and public-sector environments. It includes specialist packages at Wynn Al Marjan Island in Ras Al Khaimah, residential developments in Dubai, Dubai Exhibition Centre at Expo City and workplace projects such as EMPOWER Headquarters.

Each project page explains the identified specialist scope and names the client or developer and contractor recorded in the company profile. These references help visitors understand our experience in a particular package. They do not imply that Arcane was the main contractor for every development.

Skilled people. A considered process.

Experienced engineers, installation teams and craftspeople connect the design brief to the work on site. Our approach follows four stages: understand the project, coordinate the technical interfaces, execute the specialist work and inspect the finish.

Share the project location, type of space, specialist scope and available drawings or material requirements when starting a conversation. Our team can then discuss the relevant capabilities and the information needed to review your enquiry. Pricing, availability and delivery dates are confirmed directly with the team.
