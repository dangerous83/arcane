Sculptural gypsum for architectural expression

Sculptural GRG — glass reinforced gypsum — gives architectural forms a distinct presence within an interior. Arcane provides Sculptural GRG mould development, precision casting and installation for projects in Dubai and across the UAE, combining traditional plaster-casting skills with careful on-site assembly.

Our specialist work includes curved surfaces, decorative columns, ornate mouldings, sculptural ceilings and wall panels. These elements can define an arrival space, bring rhythm to a wall or introduce a continuous ceiling form. The choice of form and application follows the project’s design intent.

From mould development to coordinated assembly

A sculptural feature needs more than an attractive outline. Mould development, casting and assembly connect the architectural idea to the finished surface. Arcane’s process brings these stages together through specialist craftsmanship and installation coordination.

The way a feature meets the surrounding ceiling, wall or joinery is part of the design conversation. Final specifications and installation requirements are reviewed for the particular package.

Where this expertise appears in our portfolio

Wynn Al Marjan Island is a relevant company-profile reference for specialist Sculptural GRG, ceiling and wall partition works across hospitality venues. The project page separates Arcane’s package from the wider development and identifies the recorded client and contractor.

The concept imagery illustrates sculptural interior possibilities. It is not a photograph of a completed Arcane installation. For project-specific drawings, material requirements or current references, speak directly with our team.

Planning a Sculptural GRG package

Share the design drawings, project location and intended feature areas. Curved surfaces, wall panels and decorative mouldings introduce different coordination questions, so describing their relationship to the surrounding finishes is a useful starting point.

Arcane can discuss the specialist scope and the information needed to review your enquiry. Pricing, availability and programme commitments are confirmed directly for each project.

USEFUL INFORMATION

About this expertise

What does Sculptural GRG mean?

Sculptural GRG stands for glass reinforced gypsum. This capability covers mould development, casting and assembly for sculptural architectural features.

Which architectural features are included?

The published capability includes curved surfaces, decorative columns, ornate mouldings, sculptural ceilings and wall panels. Suitability and technical requirements are reviewed for the project.

Can Sculptural GRG be coordinated with other packages?

Arcane’s Sculptural GRG capability sits alongside ceiling and partition systems. Their junctions are discussed with the project team as part of technical coordination.
