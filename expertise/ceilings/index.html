CAPABILITY, PROCESS & APPLICATION

Understand the specialist work, how it connects to the complete interior and what to share for a new project enquiry.

Ceilings as part of the architecture

Arcane coordinates and installs gypsum, suspended and acoustic ceiling systems for projects in Dubai and across the UAE. Our work connects the ceiling finish with the architecture, support structure, lighting, ventilation and other embedded services.

The published capability includes gypsum, metal and acoustic systems, together with cement board, HPL, wood and stretch ceiling applications. The intended use and technical specification guide the appropriate package. A capability list does not replace project-specific system selection or performance requirements.

Technical coordination and installation

Ceiling lines, perimeter junctions and service access affect how the complete interior is detailed. Arcane’s process covers technical coordination, specialist installation and detailed inspection.

This approach is relevant to large residential developments and individual commercial or public interiors. Systems, areas and acceptance requirements are defined in discussion with architects, contractors and the relevant project teams.

Relevant ceiling references

The company profile identifies substantial gypsum ceiling packages at Burj Binghatti Jacob & Co Residences and District 01 West. It also records ceiling work across multiple residential developments in Dubai.

Residential references including Golf Ville, Golf Place and Murooj Al Furjan cover gypsum and cement board ceilings. Compare the individual project scopes; a reference does not imply that every system in our capability range was installed on that project.

Preparing a ceiling enquiry

Tell us the location, building type and systems you are considering. Drawings and the areas included help the team understand ceiling levels, service zones, access requirements and relationships with other architectural finishes.

For specifications, current availability, pricing or delivery dates, contact Arcane. The team can discuss the package and the information needed to review your project requirements.
